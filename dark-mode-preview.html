Why the old dark mode felt "off"

1 · The text was blue, not white. Headings used --navy:#d6e4ff and body used --txt:#a3b4ca — both tinted blue. On a navy page that reads icy and washed out instead of crisp.

2 · Surfaces were too close together. --bg:#0b1526 and --card:#15243b are nearly the same tone, so cards didn't separate from the page and everything looked flat.

3 · Borders were blue-grey and heavy. --line:#27385a drew visible blue outlines around every card instead of a soft hairline.

The fix: keep blue for links, icons and interactive bits only, make the text neutral, and give the surfaces a little more separation with softer borders.

Note: the hero and the "Ready for a healthier smile?" banner are intentionally dark in both themes — they're supposed to look the same in every panel.
