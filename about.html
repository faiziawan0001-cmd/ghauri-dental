About Dr. Harron Ghauri

Dr. Harron Ghauri has over 10 years of clinical experience and is committed to providing the absolute best in quality, thorough and gentle dental care for every patient. He treats his patients like family and is especially skilled in working with children, nervous patients and complex procedures.

He completed his Bachelor of Dental Surgery with high honours and has since pursued advanced training in dental implants, endodontics (painless root canal treatment) and full smile makeovers — taking pride in treating thousands of patients with care and precision.
