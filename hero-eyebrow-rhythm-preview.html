Hero eyebrow & rhythm — options

Real css/styles.css inside live frames. Only the hero is overridden.

What to look at (in this order):
  • 1. Grey vs brand. Current pill is 12% white over a dark photo, so it reads grey. Everything else in the hero is blue.
  • 2. Shape collision. A capsule at the top plus capsules at the bottom means the CTA loses its "only button here" signal.
  • 3. Vertical rhythm. Measured gaps are 16 → 44 → 26 → 22px. The widest gap sits between the headline and its own sentence, so the two never read as a pair, while the CTA sits almost against the paragraph.
  • 4. Weight tiers. The lead paragraph and the trust line are almost the same brightness, so they merge into one flat block.

Recommendation: Option A

Why the line works and the pill doesn't: your site already speaks this language — .cs-eyebrow uses gold hairlines, and the newer design previews use a rule plus overline. The hero pill is the only capsule-shaped eyebrow in the system, and a capsule reads as "tag / filter / chip" to users, not "positioning statement". A rule has none of that baggage, so the two CTAs become the only capsules on screen.

  1. Replace, don't add. Remove the fill, the 1px border, the 999px radius and the shield icon. A line + border + icon together is worse than either alone.
  2. Shorten the copy. "Family & Cosmetic Dentistry · Lahore" — drop "PK". 40 tracked caps is the reason the old code had to shrink to 9.6px on phones.
  3. Spend the colour once. Blue rule (small accent), near-white label. Keep real blue for the headline's second line and the primary CTA only — that is what creates focal hierarchy.
  4. Fix the gaps, not the alignment. The left rail is fine; the flatness comes from 16/44/26/22. Go 18 / 20 / 32 / 30 with a fading hairline above the trust rail.
  5. Then unify the sections. Every other eyebrow is still a blue-tinted pill, so ship this to .eb site-wide (leading rule for left-aligned blocks, rules on both sides for centred .sh blocks) or the hero becomes the odd one out again.

Option B is the safe fallback if you want to keep the capsule shape: brand-tinted glass instead of grey, which fixes the colour complaint but not the shape-semantics one. Option C isolates the eyebrow change so you can see exactly how much of the flatness was the pill and how much was the spacing.
