Celeste — “The Polished Pro”

Same friendly energy the widget has today, redrawn by a senior illustrator.

Safest · most familiar
Design notes

The wave, headset, clipboard and gold-trimmed coat you have now — but with cleaner proportions, glossier hair with a highlight streak, catchlit eyes, gold sneakers with white soles and a pen in the pocket. Friendly, professional, unmistakably a dental assistant.

  • Continuity: the client sees an upgrade, not a stranger.
  • Best storytelling: clipboard + headset says “helpful clinic staff” instantly.
  • Sharpest at 86 px — details stay readable.

Momo — “The Adorable Chibi”

Maximum charm: huge glossy eyes, gold star clips, navy pinafore.

Most lovable · mascot energy
Design notes

Kawaii mascot proportions — the head is half the character — with oversized sparkling eyes (double catchlights), a soft bob with gold star clips, blush-heavy cheeks and a tiny navy pinafore over white sleeves. She floats, surrounded by sparkles and a little gold heart.

  • Most memorable: people smile back at it; it reads as a brand mascot (think premium apps).
  • Emotionally magnetic — strongest “instantly friendly” effect.
  • Great as stickers in chat bubbles or promo material.

Vivienne — “The Elegant Editorial”

Waist-up fashion-illustration confidence: sleek updo, gold hoops, tailored coat.

Most luxurious · grown-up
Design notes

Poise over cuteness: a sleek low updo with a gold hairpin, winged lashes and a red lip, gold hoop earrings, and a sharply tailored white coat with the navy V, gold lapel trim, star pin and name badge. The composition fills the frame like a magazine portrait.

  • Most “$5000 website” feeling — reads as concierge-level service.
  • Best avatar crop of the five — the portrait IS the design.
  • Pairs beautifully with the gold “Most popular” tier styling.

Nova — “The Soft-Gradient Premium”

Modern product-design mascot, serenely hugging a smiling gold tooth.

Most contemporary · on-brand
Design notes

Soft volume instead of line art: gentle gradients on the coat, skin and hair, a rounded pill-shaped head, serene closed-eye smile and rosy blush — and she hugs a tiny smiling gold tooth, the only candidate with a dental sidekick. Gold gradient shoes echo the Option B buttons.

  • Most modern: the visual language of premium fintech/health apps.
  • The gold tooth is an instant clinic brand hook (usable as a chat sticker too).
  • Scales up beautifully for hero sections and print.

Aurelia — “The Luxe Badge”

A portrait seal: navy disc, double gold ring, top-knot updo. Launcher becomes a circular badge.

Most compact · jewelry-like
Design notes

Treated like a piece of jewelry: a navy gradient disc with a double gold hairline ring, and an elegant portrait inside — top-knot updo with a gold pin, winged eyes, red lip, gold studs, tailored coat with star pin. Everything is clipped to the circle, so it is razor-sharp at any size.

  • Most compact & premium — a “luxury seal” instead of a cartoon.
  • Perfect avatar: the same artwork serves launcher + header + favicon-style uses.
  • Option: keep the standing layout by placing the bust next to the pill instead.
